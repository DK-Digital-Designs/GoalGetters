How Joining Works

1) Book a Trial

Choose an open session or Trials Week. Bring boots, water, and a good attitude.

2) Coach Feedback

We assess technical level, work rate, and fit. You’ll get clear next steps within 48 hours.

3) Register & Start

Pick your program, collect kit, and join your squad’s training slots.

FAQs

What does a trial include?

Warm-up, technical station, small-sided game. Coaches evaluate attitude, effort, and basics.

What are the fees?

See Programs & Fees above. Kit priced separately; payment plans available.

Where are sessions held?

Randburg Sports Complex; match venues vary by league fixtures.

Which age groups?

U13, U15, U17, Senior. We place players where they’ll develop fastest.
